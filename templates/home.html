Our Mission

Equipping young minds to thrive and sustain communities.

To equip children and young people, particularly those from underserved communities, with the emotional intelligence, practical skills, mentorship, career readiness, and leadership capabilities they need to thrive while creating resilient, inclusive, and sustainable communities.

Our Vision

A world where every young person builds a future with purpose.

A world where every child and young person has the opportunity to realise their full potential, lead purposeful lives, and build thriving, inclusive, and sustainable communities.

The Foundation

Our Core Belief

"Talent is everywhere Opportunity starts here."

We also believe that communities already possess untapped potential—in their people, ideas, and resources. By equipping children and young people with the skills, confidence, and opportunities to solve real-world challenges, we create lasting change that benefits individuals, families, and entire communities.

Our Approach

At Revled Foundation, we believe meaningful change happens when young people are equipped not only with knowledge but also with the confidence, practical skills, mentorship, and opportunities to apply what they learn to real-life challenges.

01

Learn

We nurture emotional intelligence, confidence, leadership, and essential life skills that enable children and young people to make informed decisions and build positive relationships.

02

Create

We provide practical, vocational, digital, and entrepreneurial learning experiences that help participants discover their talents and develop skills for employment, enterprise, and lifelong success.

03

Innovate

We encourage curiosity, creativity, problem-solving, and the use of technology to develop solutions that address everyday challenges within homes, schools, and communities.

04

Transform

We help young people recognise value where others see limitations by encouraging responsible resource use, repurposing donated materials and technology, and creating opportunities that benefit both people and communities.

05

Thrive

We support participants to transition confidently into education, employment, entrepreneurship, and leadership while contributing to inclusive, resilient, and sustainable communities.

Our Programmes

Every Revled programme is designed around one purpose: equipping children and young people with the skills, confidence, experiences, and opportunities to thrive as they solve real challenges in their communities. From leadership and career readiness to entrepreneurship, innovation, and responsible resource use, our programmes prepare participants to build brighter futures for themselves and those around them.

ReCreate Labs

We encourage participants to see value where others see waste. By repurposing donated technology, fabrics, furniture, and other materials into learning tools, marketable products, and community assets, participants develop practical skills while contributing to cleaner, stronger, and more resourceful communities.
